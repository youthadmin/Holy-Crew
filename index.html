WHAT IS HOLY CREW

LEADERSHIP STARTS BEFORE THE LIGHTS COME ON

Every Wednesday, before Youth ever begins, a group of students shows up early. They pray over the room. They get trained. Then they host, serve, and lead their peers all night long. That group is Holy Crew.

Holy Crew is a year-long journey of spiritual formation and practical leadership. You will be trained, mentored, and given real ownership in what God is doing at Mannahouse Youth. This is not a hangout. It is a commitment, and it will change you.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

REAL TRAINING. REAL OWNERSHIP.

01

BE FORMED

Weekly teaching on character, calling, and what it actually means to lead like Jesus. Foundation first, platform second.

02

SERVE ON A TEAM

Hosting, worship, prayer, production, and more. You will find your lane and serve on it every single Wednesday at Youth.

03

LEAD YOUR PEERS

Holy Crew sets the temperature of the room on Wednesday nights and carries that influence into schools and friendships.

04

GROW TOGETHER

You will not do this alone. Holy Crew is a tight community of students and leaders pushing each other toward Jesus.

THE SCHEDULE

4:30 UNTIL THE END OF YOUTH

Holy Crew is not just a meeting before service. Crew trains first, then serves through the entire night of Youth. You show up early and you stay until the end. That is the commitment.

4:30

SHOW UP

Doors open. Check in, lock in, and pray over the night together.

4:30 TO 5:30

GET TRAINED

Leadership formation, team huddles, and prep for the night ahead.

YOUTH

SERVE THE NIGHT

Crew is on. Hosting, worship, prayer, production. You carry the room.

THE END

FINISH STRONG

Crew stays until Youth is done. Reset the room, debrief, go home full.

FOR PARENTS

WHAT YOUR STUDENT IS SIGNING UP FOR

Holy Crew is a faith-based leadership development program for high school students, led by the Mannahouse Youth pastoral team. Students arrive Wednesdays at 4:30 PM for training and mentorship, then serve throughout our regular Youth service until it ends.

Our goal is simple: to partner with you in raising students of character who know who they are in Christ. If you have any questions about the program, expectations, or safety, reach out to the youth office and we will be glad to talk.
